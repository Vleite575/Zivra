# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Young people aged 14–25 in Brazil, using Zivra in their free time, mostly on their phones. They post short photos and videos from their everyday lives and follow friends and creators.

## Product Purpose
Zivra is a social network for sharing photos and short videos with the people you choose. Success means users post regularly and come back to see what their friends shared.

## Positioning
Zivra is a Brazilian network built for smaller, closer circles. Private profiles require approval before anyone can follow them. The feed is chronological and shows only public profiles, people you follow, and your own posts. There is no aggressive recommendation algorithm.

## Operating Context
Mobile-first use in short sessions. The interface is in Brazilian Portuguese. Minimum age is 14, enforced at signup by birth date.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Accounts: signup (name, username, email, birth date, password), login, password reset.
- Posts: text up to 280 characters with optional image (jpg/png) or video (mp4/mov/avi, up to 20 MB). Likes and comments.
- Profiles: photo, bio up to 160 characters, public or private. Follow requests for private profiles can be accepted or rejected.
- Followers and following lists. Only the profile owner can sort them by date.
- Not available: direct messages/chat (removed until a backend exists), feed pagination (capped at 50 posts), email verification.
- Stack: Next.js frontend in `web/`, Laravel JSON API.

## Brand Commitments
The name "Zivra" is fixed. Logo, colors, and typography are open: the old identity (indigo accent, Breeze defaults) is not binding.

## Evidence on Hand
No real user content, testimonials, metrics, or press exist. Do not fabricate user counts, quotes, or reviews.

## Product Principles
1. Your circle first: people you chose come before anything recommended.
2. Privacy is a visible control, not a buried setting.
3. Posting is fast and light: one thought, one photo or clip.
4. Speak like a Brazilian friend, never like a corporation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Audience includes minors (14+). Keep safety and privacy controls clear. Follow WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access.
